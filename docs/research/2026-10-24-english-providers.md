# English chapter-image providers for Bee's Reader

Accessed **2026-10-24**. Scope: Next.js on Vercel, browser-local profiles, no managed database, and chapters rendered as images in Bee's Reader's continuous vertical reader. This note evaluates evidence of integration permission and technical interfaces; it does not make a legal determination. No accounts were created, payments made, providers contacted, or protected paid chapters fetched.

## Finding

**Comick.dev passed a limited live English-image test, but did not solve the sampled mainstream-title gaps. MangaPDF was unavailable. No additional provider met all the production criteria: demonstrated relevant English coverage, usable page delivery, and sufficiently established third-party image-use conditions.** Both publish public developer interfaces; reviewed sources leave content permission questions unresolved. The commercial publishers/platforms reviewed do not publish a verified self-service chapter-image integration matching this requirement. A free preview, accessible image URL, subscription, or functioning endpoint does not itself document permission for third-party display.

MangaDex remains the existing primary-provider decision; this research does not re-certify its catalog rights or convert external-only chapters into images. AniList/Jikan remain metadata-only. In particular, the existing English Solo Leveling MangaDex external-only finding is unchanged.

## Public-API candidates: what the documentation actually proves

### Comick.dev

The [developer landing page](https://comick.dev/docs) is an affirmative integration invitation and distinguishes unauthenticated catalog access from OAuth library access. It does **not** establish that Bee's Reader may display every returned image/title. The site's [About](https://comick.dev/about) calls it a tracker/wiki/community; its [terms](https://comick.dev/terms) and [DMCA page](https://comick.dev/dmca) do not provide a reviewed chain of title/translation licenses. These pages neither prove infringement nor establish English image redistribution rights.

Its [library errors/limits](https://comick.dev/docs/errors) specify 60 reads/minute per app/user, 1,000/minute per app and 120/minute per network address, `Retry-After`, and private/no-store responses. **Those are library limits, not verified catalog/image limits.** A shared Vercel outbound address can aggregate traffic. Local-profile reading should not add account OAuth merely because it exists.

### MangaPDF

The [API overview](https://docs.mangapdf.org/guide/api/index.html) documents `https://api.coffeemanga.shop` and alternative `https://api.mangapdf.org`, identical catalogs, mandatory `X-Client: api-consumer`, and these read routes:

```text
GET /api/v1/mihon/popular?page=1
GET /api/v1/mihon/search?q=...&page=1
GET /api/v1/mihon/manga/{id}
GET /api/v1/mihon/chapter/{id}/pages
GET /images/{path}
```

Pagination is 40 items with `has_next`; missing/invalid client header returns 403. The docs permit building web readers and do not require attribution. No reviewed source establishes underlying title/translation rights, English coverage, image retention rights, or service guarantees. Direct document fetches returned 521 through the web tool and 403 through Python; the first-party search index exposed the overview. Treat indexed documentation and live service availability as separate evidence. The [reference](https://docs.mangapdf.org/guide/api/reference.html) was available through its indexed first-party search result but not a direct live fetch.

## Internal endpoint evidence is not a public API agreement

The public [MANGA Plus frontend bundle](https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/js/app.41982a95.js), linked by its [homepage](https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/), sets `https://jumpg-webapi.tokyo-cdn.com/api` as its API base and references `/title_list/allV2`, `/title_detailV4`, and `/manga_viewer_v3`. This is first-party implementation evidence only; these reader routes were not invoked for this research. No supported external auth/limits/rights contract was found, and changing internal versions make a production adapter fragile.

GlobalComix's [consumer terms](https://globalcomix.com/terms-of-use) license personal, noncommercial viewing through its service; [publisher terms](https://globalcomix.com/publisher-terms-of-service) address publishing **onto GlobalComix**, not a public downstream distribution API. Existing publisher partnerships do not establish a partner program that supplies images to Bee's Reader.

## Decision and remaining validation

There is no currently validated commercial-provider drop-in for English chapters in our own reader. The Comick tests below establish limited image delivery, not broad title coverage or permission for every returned release. MangaPDF remains operationally unverified beyond its failing health endpoints. A directly authorized creator/publisher feed is another possible route, but no such feed was supplied or verified here.

Before promoting any candidate, obtain evidence covering third-party image display, original/translated work rights, countries, free versus paid entitlements, caching/proxying/hotlinking, attribution, takedowns and service termination. Then verify page order, dimensions, CORS/referrer rules, signed-URL lifetime, rate limits and actual Vercel behavior. Browser-local profiles solve reading-state storage; they do not replace upstream entitlements or confer content permission. An authorized stateless feed could fit the architecture without a managed database; a provider that requires durable account/entitlement synchronization needs a separate design decision.

## Live technical evidence

Machine-readable results: [2026-10-24-provider-probes.json](./2026-10-24-provider-probes.json). Repeatable probe: `scripts/check-provider-candidates.ts`. A new run writes `provider-probes-latest.json` and preserves this dated historical record.

```sh
node --conditions=react-server --import tsx scripts/check-provider-candidates.ts
```

This command is a research report generator, **not a production integration test suite**: exit zero means observations were recorded; inspect each `outcome` and the image assertion result. It requires the normal `npm ci` dependencies and no keys or accounts. It uses anonymous public GETs, 1.1-second per-origin pacing, 12-second timeouts, a 2MB metadata limit, and cooldowns on 429/5xx. It does not automatically retry or defeat browser challenges. One first-page image at most is fetched by the CLI, decoded, hashed, and discarded. No image files or complete chapter archives are saved.

The [Comick catalog explorer](https://api.comick.dev/docs/#description/quick-start) was rendered in the ordinary in-app browser after a direct Node request to its documentation path received a 403 challenge. No CAPTCHA was solved. Its documented flow uses versioned search/detail/chapter-list endpoints, then `/chapter/{hid}/get_images`. The image endpoint supplies ordered records, not guaranteed complete URLs. The CDN mapping was independently observed on the first-page `img src` in the [provider's public chapter reader](https://comick.dev/comic/is-she-making-fun-of-me/LcG1AnYc-chapter-13.2-en), then tested with our server fetcher. The product's existing DNS/public-IP/HTTPS protections were reused unchanged.

Commercial providers in the comparison were reviewed through first-party documentation/terms; **their protected chapter APIs were not invoked**. The earlier MangaDex live checks remain in `docs/live-integration-results.json` and `docs/vercel-production-smoke.json`; they were not repeated for this candidate survey. All new network probes ran locally, not from a deployed Vercel function. No complete series-availability audit, account/subscription flows, load test, signed-URL expiry test or territorial comparison was performed.

## Recommendation for Bee's Reader

1. Keep MangaDex as the enabled reader source. Show actual hosted English availability so metadata-only titles are not mistaken for readable content.
2. Treat Comick as a **limited technical candidate**, not a confirmed replacement or Solo Leveling fix. Clarify its image-display/proxy/cache/attribution conditions and test the actual titles users want before implementing an adapter. Do not promise all 817 release entries have pages.
3. Hold MangaPDF until its origin is healthy and the permitted image use and English coverage can be verified. Do not implement aggressive host switching or retry loops around its outage.
4. For a dependable wider catalog within our own reader, pursue a directly authorized publisher/creator feed or support local user-supplied CBZ files. Neither option automatically supplies missing mainstream English releases.

No production provider was added, no personal profile storage changed, and no database or login requirement was introduced. If a new adapter is approved later, current MangaDex UUID-only routes/storage schemas must become provider-aware because Comick uses HIDs. Preserve language filtering, release-group attribution, independent page availability, explicit quality capabilities, image SSRF controls, bounded transfer/caching and shared-IP throttling. Comick's image schema does not expose MangaDex's `data-saver` contract; do not label original-only delivery as data-saver.
